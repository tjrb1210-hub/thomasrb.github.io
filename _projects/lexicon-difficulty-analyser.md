## Overview

The Lexicon Difficulty Analyser is a personal programming project exploring how the complexity of English vocabulary might be assessed computationally.

Using Python, the project examines written text and identifies vocabulary that may be more challenging for language learners.

## What it explores

- Word frequency and rarity
- Word length and syllable count
- Vocabulary difficulty and language proficiency
- Applications in language learning and teaching

## Future development

Future work will explore more sophisticated vocabulary measures, visual representations of results and potential applications in language education.
